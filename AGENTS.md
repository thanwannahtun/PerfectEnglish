## What Perfect English is and what to Do ?
Perfect English is an App that students, professionals, and anyone looking to improve their English can use to practice speaking, writing, grammar, and conversational skills. The app provides interactive exercises, real-time feedback, and personalized learning paths.

### 2. Styling

### 3. Take a Quiz
- Every Grammar section must include a quiz to test the user's understanding of the concepts.
- Always Use the existing quiz widgets or components to create the quizzes, and ensure they are interactive and provide immediate feedback to the user.

### 4. Burmese <-> English
- Always pair English with Burmese translation for every explanation, example, and quiz question to ensure that users can understand the content in their native language.
